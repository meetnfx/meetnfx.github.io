01 / GEORGIA TECH · CS 7641 · MACHINE LEARNING

Out-of-stock grocery recommendation engine

A team project that recommends personalized substitutes when grocery products are unavailable. Combined Instacart shopping histories and Open Food Facts attributes with Gaussian Mixture Model product families, graph-based candidate generation, and an XGBoost model for substitute selection.

The final report records ROC-AUC 0.9601 and F1 0.8364 on replay-based evaluation, using simulated out-of-stock labels rather than observed customer acceptance. A React and D3 interface lets visitors explore product clusters and recommendations.

My contribution: Data preprocessing and cleaning, exploratory analysis, model evaluation, interactive UI and visualization, and the final report and presentation.

04 / PUBLICATION

Making risk assessment more consistent

CO-AUTHORED PUBLICATION

An integrated RFUCOM–RTOPSIS approach for failure modes and effects analysis: A case of manufacturing industry

Reliability Engineering & System Safety · Volume 221 · 2022 · Article 108333

This research was part of my M.Tech project in Industrial Engineering at IIT Kharagpur.

How can engineers prioritize equipment failure modes when expert judgments are uncertain? This study combines rough-number theory, the Full Consistency Method, and TOPSIS to weight risk criteria and rank failure modes.

Method

The framework represents uncertainty in expert assessments and accounts for the relative importance of risk criteria, addressing limitations of conventional risk-priority-number scoring.

Application

Applied to a thin slab caster and rolling unit in an integrated steel plant, with comparative and sensitivity analyses to assess the method’s effectiveness and robustness.
